# Algebra Tiles

Shareable practice activities where students multiply and factor polynomials with algebra tiles, set up by a teacher and opened by students from a link.

## Language

### Tiles

**Algebra tile**:
A flat piece whose area stands for one term: an x² tile, an x tile or a unit tile, each positive or negative.
_Avoid_: Block, piece, manipulative

**x² tile**:
A square tile, x long on each side, standing for x².

**x tile**:
A long tile, x long and 1 wide, standing for x. It may lie across or stand up.

**Unit tile**:
A small square tile, 1 on each side, standing for 1.
_Avoid_: 1 tile, one tile, small square

**Negative tile**:
A tile standing for the opposite of its positive twin, shown in red.

**Zero pair**:
A positive tile and a negative tile of the same kind, which together stand for zero.

**Zero out**:
To take a zero pair off the board, once its two tiles lie flush against each other: the zero principle, a number and its opposite make zero.
_Avoid_: Cancel out, delete, remove (for a zero pair)

### The mat

**Mat**:
The working surface: a T whose two arms are the sides, and the rectangle inside it.
_Avoid_: Board, frame, T-chart

**Side**:
One arm of the T, where a factor is laid out in x tiles and unit tiles, in one row from the corner: the top side or the left side.
_Avoid_: Edge, dimension, axis

**Rectangle**:
The region inside the T where the product is built, one tile where each row meets each column.
_Avoid_: Area, product area, box

**Like-term group**:
A labelled place where the student gathers every tile of one kind from the rectangle and zeroes out its zero pairs: the x² group, the x group or the unit group.
_Avoid_: Bin, pile, bucket

**Starting tiles**:
The tiles a student finds beside the mat when an activity opens, as the teacher chose: all the tiles they need, only the positive ones, or none.

**Tray**:
The fixed shelf of tiles the student takes new tiles from, one of each kind, flipped over to show the positive or the negative ones.
_Avoid_: Palette, bank, supply

**Board**:
The whiteboard the mat lies on, which the student pans and zooms; tiles may lie anywhere on it.
_Avoid_: Canvas, workspace

### Activities

**Activity**:
One problem a teacher shares with students as a link, with its activity type.
_Avoid_: Assignment, preset, problem set

**Problem**:
The expression an activity asks about: two factors in Multiply, a polynomial in Factor.
_Avoid_: Question, equation (for the given expression)

**Activity type**:
The kind of practice an activity is: Multiply or Factor.
_Avoid_: Mode

**Multiply**:
The activity type in which the student is given two factors and builds their product with tiles.

**Factor**:
The activity type in which the student is given a polynomial and builds it into a rectangle whose sides are its factors.
_Avoid_: Factoring mode
